## Found while implementing (fixed in plan 008 C)

- **`member-keys` had no writer rule.** The testing store let any authenticated account publish keys under another account's id, and "newest published + 1" could be exhausted by one record at the largest version. D-43 gained two server rules: only the account writes its keys, at its next version. §11, scope rule 8, the integration guide and the testing store follow.

## Accepted, stated in the specs

- A PIN or phrase thief with a session can still take the account over; the remedy is a support restore (TH-11, SP-26).
- A version restore keeps owner approvals made in between, and the testing store also restores records the thief made at that version (for example unproven re-keys): the user secures the account right after the restore (key hierarchy, "Restoring an account").

No divergence left between threats, properties, protocol and code for this plan's scope. Not re-reviewed: the parts of the specs the plan didn't touch (covered by review 001).
