## Findings

### A. A local anchor nobody verified becomes every bundle's floor, and survives `secureAccount`

- **Kind:** forgeable input, combined attack. **Severity:** medium (two actors for a forged team; one actor for a lasting loss of the team; a documented remedy leaves the user compromised).
- **Where:** D-50 ("the highest of the team's bundles' floors, the local anchor and the account's `team-state` anchor"), §4.3, §5.7 step 4, §5.9; `src/account/recovery.ts:79-94` (`newestTrustedAnchor`), called by `rekeyTeam` (`:70`) and `rotateAccount` (`rotation.ts:55`).
- **Evidence (reproduced):** `newestTrustedAnchor` takes the highest of the bundles' floors, the `LocalKeyStore` anchor and the `team-state` anchor, without checking any of them against a chain. The local anchor is the one input there whose producers include A7 and A9 on the device (forgeability table): in the browser it is AES-GCM under a non-extractable key the page can still use. Before plan 009 a bad local anchor stopped only that device; D-48 copied it into a floorless bundle at a re-key or rotation, and D-50 copies it into every bundle of the team at every re-key and rotation, wrapped under the account key: every device and every receiving context then trusts it.
  - **Alone (A9 or A7 on the vault's device):** the script writes a local anchor `{version 50, random hash}` for the team. Bob runs `secureAccount` from that device, the documented remedy for TH-20. Every bundle's floor is now version 50. A clean new device, honest server: the floor is above every other anchor, so it counts, and the open fails with `TRUST_FAILURE` (`rollback`). A support restore of `team-state` doesn't help: the floor is inside `member-keys-wrap` at the new account key version.
  - **With A2:** the local anchor is the hash of a version 9 the server forged (owned by mallory, rostering bob's real keys). After `secureAccount`, the floor is that anchor. A new device of bob's, `team-state` withheld and the forged version 9 served: it opens `ready` at version 9, so what it writes is readable by the server. This is TH-04 brought back after the join, and after the remedy.
- **Fix proposed:** the floor comes only from what the account authenticated: the bundles' floors and the `team-state` anchor, not the local anchor. `team-state` is written only by `advanceAnchor`, from a verified chain, under a `teamStateKey` no actor outside the account's sessions holds (§4.4); the server can only serve an older revision, which the maximum ignores. Rewrite D-50's write rule (or a new decision amending it), §4.3, §5.7 step 4, §5.9, §6.8 step 1, SP-07 text; drop `local` from `newestTrustedAnchor`; keep both throwaway cases as tests (refused, and the floor unchanged by a local anchor), and prove the guard by putting `local` back.
  - Cost: a floor can lag the device's own newest anchor by one when its `team-state` write lost every compare-and-set retry; the next open writes it. No download, no layout change.
  - Alternative, not recommended: keep the local anchor but run §6.1 steps 1–2 at each re-key and rotation and take the verified tip as the floor. It refuses a bad local anchor (the team goes to `failed`), but costs one chain download per team at every rotation, and puts a chain fetch into `secureAccount`.
- **Needs:** a decision (amends D-50).
